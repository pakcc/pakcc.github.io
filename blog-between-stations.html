Between StationsA note from the road

There is a kind of time that belongs only to the journey itself.

SAMPLE ENTRY / DATE TO ADD

A train window turns the world into a moving collection of almosts: a field almost familiar, a town almost known, a face almost remembered.

I like the stretch between leaving and arriving. Nothing is required of me there except to look out, listen, and let the landscape change. The next city has not yet become a set of streets I know. The last one is already becoming a story.

Perhaps that is why I keep tickets. They record a small promise: for a little while, I was going somewhere. The journey may have been ordinary, but it belonged to a particular day and a particular version of me.

This is demo writing. Replace it with a journey, date, and memory that are yours.
