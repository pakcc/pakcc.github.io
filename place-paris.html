PARISFrance · Paris

Paris makes me want to slow my steps: walk along the river, hear cups clink on a street corner, and let the evening last a little longer.

← BACK TO PLACES

Paris is like a letter left half written, too dear to send.

To me, Paris is stone walls warmed by afternoon sun, the scent of a bakery drifting around a corner, and the Seine gathering the fading light. Its beauty does not ask me to hurry; even a walk feels like an arrival.

When dusk settles on the rooftops, the city grows a little quieter. I want to take one more street and put off leaving for just a while.
